AniSkip

Crowd-sourced anime opening, ending and recap skip times from the AniSkip database (api.aniskip.com), published as Jellyfin media segments. AniDB/AniList ids on your anime are mapped to MyAnimeList ids via arm.haglund.dev. Run the "Detect and Analyze Media Segments" task after changing settings.
